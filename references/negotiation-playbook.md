# SEELE creator negotiation playbook

Use this reference after an explicit creator quote is captured and the evidence review in `quote-review.md` establishes the format, Brief fit, relevant median views, CPM/CPV, rights, and commercially reasonable range.

## Authorization and stopping rules

- Analyze every explicit quote and propose at least one value-preserving negotiation exchange.
- Do not negotiate a Dedicated Video or Sponsored Integration quote at or above USD 1,000. Mark it `Pass` unless the user explicitly grants a creator-specific exception.
- Every first-round counter and second-round counter 发送前都必须把完整话术给用户审批. Keep it unsent and mark `第一轮待确认` or `第二轮待确认` until the user approves it in the current task. General permission to reply or send outreach does not replace this price-message approval.
- Do not auto-accept, sign, promise a schedule, grant rights, or send a Brief at any amount.
- Stop after a rejected second-round floor. Recommend accepting the creator's floor, changing scope, or stopping; do not continue indefinite pressure.
- Preserve the original quote and every counter with timestamps. Never rewrite quote history to show only the latest amount.

## Format priority

- Default to `Dedicated Video` because SEELE needs a complete, credible build-and-play workflow.
- Use `Sponsored Integration` only when Dedicated pricing or execution is not supportable.
- Record Dedicated and Integration prices, counters, deliverables, and outcomes separately. Do not use the lower format to hide or overwrite the higher quote.

## Build the commercially reasonable range

Show the evidence supporting both the lower and upper bound:

- recent relevant non-pinned median views and sample size;
- estimated CPM/CPV and the applicable reference range;
- content and audience fit;
- real Workspace demonstration, iteration, and playtest production effort;
- revisions, pre-publication review, schedule, and reliability;
- organic publishing, reposting, paid usage, whitelisting, raw files, edits, territory, and exclusivity;
- tax, platform fees, prepayment, cancellation, and postponement terms;
- the creator's first quote, later counteroffers, and concessions already made.

Unknown terms remain `待确认`. A percentage anchor never substitutes for evidence.

## Price ladder

- Far above the evidence-supported ceiling: first counter normally 50%–65% of the quote.
- Moderately above the ceiling: first counter normally 70%–80% of the quote.
- Inside the reasonable range: reduce 5%–15%, or hold price and request additional value such as clearer review, revisions, timing, or organic reposting.
- Already unusually low: do not force a commercially irrational reduction. Keep the price and exchange it for clearer scope, review, revisions, timing, or rights.

The objective is the lowest commercially reasonable and executable price, not the lowest number regardless of delivery quality.

## Voluntarily supplied phone numbers

- Use a country code only when the creator voluntarily includes the number in a business conversation.
- Treat it as a possible phone-registration region, not proof of residence, audience location, production cost, or payment entity.
- Cross-check the channel location, content language, company information, or creator self-description. Record the sources and confidence.
- Regional market evidence may make the target price 最多额外下调 15% compared with the evidence-based target before the adjustment.
- Never use the country code as the sole pricing factor, never mention GDP-based pricing to the creator, do not store the full number, and 不通过 WhatsApp 联系 the creator.
- Record only `可能地区`, `地区证据来源`, `地区证据可信度`, and `地区调整幅度`.

## Scope binding

Every counteroffer must name the exact format and retain the applicable Brief hard gates from `quote-review.md`.

For a Dedicated Video, the default counter covers:

- at least six minutes total;
- at least 60% and at least four minutes of valid SEELE content;
- SEELE AI and the task within the first 30 seconds;
- real Workspace operation within the first 25% of the video;
- `input → Workspace → generation → meaningful iteration → playable result` with corresponding evidence;
- final playable result, one CTA, tracking link, and paid-partnership disclosure;
- unlisted pre-publication review and two reasonable revision rounds.

For a Sponsored Integration fallback, the default counter covers:

- one continuous independent 60–90 second segment;
- the segment in the first 35% of the video and no later than five minutes;
- SEELE AI and the task within the first 10 seconds of the segment;
- at least 30 seconds of real Workspace operation and at least 10 seconds of playable result;
- one CTA, tracking link, paid-partnership disclosure, unlisted review, and two reasonable revision rounds.

Confirm organic publication, retention period, 30-day organic reposting on SEELE-owned channels, paid usage, whitelisting, raw files, edits, territory, exclusivity, schedule, payment, tax, fees, and cancellation separately. Never assume an unstated right is included.

## 第一轮 / First-round counter

After the user approves the complete populated first-round message in the current task, use verified creator-specific values in this template:

```text
Hi [Name],

Thank you for sharing your rate. We’re genuinely interested in working with you, especially because your content around [content direction] aligns well with the practical build-and-play demonstration we want to test.

For this initial collaboration, based on the confirmed [Dedicated Video / Sponsored Integration] scope, recent public performance, and our pilot budget, would you be able to do this for USD [First Counteroffer]?

The proposed rate would cover [scope summary], including a real SEELE workflow from input and Workspace operation through generation, meaningful iteration, and a playable result. It would also include a clear CTA and tracking link, paid-partnership disclosure, an unlisted pre-publication review, and two reasonable revision rounds.

Please also confirm the expected turnaround time and whether 30-day organic reposting on SEELE-owned channels is included.

Best,
Shaohui
SEELE AI
```

Do not send if any runtime field is still bracketed, unsupported, or inconsistent with the thread.

## 第二轮 / Second-round counter

Prepare this only when the first response remains above the reasonable low target or changes the scope. Show the complete populated message to the user before sending.

```text
Hi [Name],

Thank you for considering our proposal. We remain very interested in working with you and believe your channel is a strong fit for SEELE AI.

After reviewing the campaign scope and current pilot budget, the best rate we can propose for this initial collaboration is USD [Final Counteroffer] for [confirmed format and deliverables]. The previously discussed deliverables and review requirements would remain unchanged.

If you can confirm this rate and the agreed scope, our next step will be to finalize the written deliverables and move the collaboration into [formal confirmation / internal approval]. Please let us know if you can make this work.

Best,
Shaohui
SEELE AI
```

- Use `formal confirmation` only when the final price is at or below USD 500.
- Use `internal approval` when the final price is above USD 500.
- Do not mechanically repeat already confirmed requirements. List only the retained scope and unresolved terms.

## Approval

- Quote at or above USD 1,000 for either format: `Pass` before negotiation, unless the user explicitly approves an exception.
- Final price at or below USD 500: `可按团队流程推进`, but actual acceptance, signature, schedule, Brief, product access, payment, or rights still requires explicit authorization.
- Final price above USD 500 and below USD 1,000: `待团队审批`; never imply approval before it exists.

## Required negotiation output

Report the original quote, format, reasonable range and evidence, first counter and response, second counter and approval state, final amount, retained deliverables, unresolved rights/terms, possible region evidence and adjustment, current approval status, and next action.
