# SEELE creator sourcing rules

## First principle

Do not search for “small gaming influencers” as an end in itself. Find creators who can make target users believe: “I can use SEELE to turn my idea into a playable game.” Optimize for low-cost learning and explainable experiments, not one speculative viral hit.

## Default contract

- Platforms: TikTok and YouTube, including Shorts.
- Export target: 200 qualified, directly contactable creators per run.
- Median-view gate: at least 5,000 across recent relevant, non-pinned content.
- Follower count: no hard gate; normally prioritize 5,000–300,000 while allowing low-follower, high-view creators.
- Markets: English content; prioritize United States, United Kingdom, and Australia. India, Argentina, and other English/Spanish markets can remain when content and audience fit are strong.
- Outreach: an exported profile does not count as an outreach KPI. Count only messages with a confirmed sent record.
- Budget context: monthly spend is USD 20,000; a final negotiated price above USD 500 requires team review. A price at or below USD 500 may proceed through the team's normal process, but sourcing or price status never authorizes automatic acceptance. Never promise price during sourcing.

## Six review dimensions

### UGC delivery ability

Look for on-camera speaking, voice-over, screen recording, coherent 15–45 second editing, hooks, pacing, captions, and consistent publishing. A high posting frequency made of reposts does not demonstrate delivery ability.

### Game-creation motivation

Strong signals: Devlog, Game Jam, Mod, mechanics remake, level design, Unity, Unreal Engine, Godot, Roblox/Minecraft creation, AI game building, or no-code game creation. Playing games, highlights, esports, memes, and trailer reposting are not creation signals.

### Audience overlap

Target independent developers, players who want to make games, no-code creators, AI-tool users, and people discussing design, mechanics, prompts, engines, or workflows. Generic praise, esports discussion, and character fandom do not prove overlap.

### Product persuasiveness

The creator must be capable of showing: idea → prompt/input → generated prototype → real play → adjustment → updated playable result. Generic praise or scripted claims without operation and proof are weak.

### Unit communication efficiency

Use the median of relevant non-pinned posts, comment quality, interaction relative to audience size, and stability. A-grade evidence uses 6–10 relevant posts. A B-grade replenishment lead may use 4–5 relevant posts when the 5K median-view gate and every contact, deduplication, activity, safety, and content-fit gate pass; label the smaller sample explicitly. Do not use follower count or one viral post alone.

### Commercial executability

Check English delivery, active account, reachable public business channel, content safety, willingness to try a new tool, ability to follow an agreed shooting method, revisions, basic data return, and reasonable usage-right discussions.

## Three experiment layers

Record each creator on three independent layers:

1. Creator category: game-development, AI/tech, productivity, no-code/creative-tool, narrative, lifestyle, or another explicit category.
2. Shooting method: talking head, screen recording, person + screen recording, narrative skit, or documented experience.
3. Content angle: result-first, identity, old-workflow comparison, specific scenario, real demonstration, or objection handling.

First-round priority: real demonstration, old-workflow comparison, result-first, and one specific scenario. Identity and objection-handling add creator-persona variables and belong later unless a clear reason justifies them.

## Experiment value

A creator is worth test spend when they:

- represent an explicit creator category not yet tested;
- can follow the selected shooting method rather than only a fixed ad template;
- can run SEELE and show the real process;
- reach target creators rather than generic entertainment traffic;
- can return basic views, effective views/completion where available, interactions, clicks, or registrations;
- change only one or two variables relative to a comparison group;
- offer learning value proportionate to price.

Avoid high exploratory spend when creator category, shooting method, and content angle all change; when only generic voice-over is possible; when the audience is too broad; when data/revisions/rights are absent; or when the creator duplicates an existing test.

## Grade

- A: strong content/audience fit, real-demo capability, useful experiment slot, and reachable.
- B: worth a low-cost test but one material fact needs human confirmation.
- C: low priority; do not count toward the default 200 unless the user requests a reserve pool.
- Pass: rejected, with a specific reason.

Weighting:

- Content and SEELE fit: 30%
- Recent view stability: 20%
- Real demonstration and narrative ability: 15%
- Audience and comment quality: 15%
- Market/language fit: 10%
- Contact completeness: 5%
- Brand safety: 5%

## Non-negotiable safeguards

- Never invent an email, country, audience demographic, view count, or representative content URL.
- Never lower the content-fit, valid-link, contact-channel, or 5K median-view gates to reach 200.
- The daily send-ready list may include A and B. B is not a reserve-only grade, but it must pass the public-business-email, deduplication, activity, safety, content-fit, and 5K median-view gates and disclose its uncertainty.
- Count clearly linked cross-platform accounts as one Creator ID by default.
- Direct outreach is allowed when the user explicitly authorizes sending and the recipient, public business contact, and historical deduplication checks pass.
- Disclose when no historical list was available; in that case only in-batch deduplication is proven.
- Record evidence links and verification dates for factual judgments.
