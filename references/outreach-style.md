# Outbound first-contact style

## Contract

- Write 3–5 concise English sentences.
- Truthfully reference one specific creator work or recurring content format.
- Explain one natural SEELE-to-audience connection.
- Default to a paid dedicated YouTube video and ask for the creator's current Dedicated rate and upcoming availability.
- Keep Sponsored Integration as a fallback when Dedicated pricing or execution is unsuitable. Record and negotiate the formats separately; do not ask the creator to collapse both into one price.
- Do not promise price, usage rights, posting date, performance, or approval.
- Do not send a full Brief or affiliate program in the first message.
- When the user explicitly authorizes sending, the verified first-contact message may be sent directly after recipient and duplicate checks. Treat only a confirmed sent record as successful outreach.
- If personalization cannot be verified, write a factual category-level draft and mark it for human revision.

## Canonical example

```text
Subject: Paid YouTube Collaboration with SEELE AI

Hi Alex,

I came across your video “Building My First Unity Prototype” and liked how you showed the playable result before explaining the build process. I’m working with SEELE AI, a platform that helps creators turn an idea into a playable game and iterate on it through a real build-and-play workflow. We think this could fit naturally with your audience and would love to explore a paid dedicated YouTube video. If you’re interested, could you share your current rate and upcoming availability for a dedicated video?

Best,
Shaohui
SEELE AI
```

Bad personalization includes “I love your content,” references to videos that were not opened, or claims about audience demographics without evidence.
