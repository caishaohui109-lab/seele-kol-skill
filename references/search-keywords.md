## Adjacent-range rule

Use adjacent pools to increase volume: AI-tool demonstrations, coding-project builders, no-code/app builders, creative automation, 3D/animation workflows, interactive storytelling, virtual-world creation, and product-prototyping channels. Do not accept generic AI news, passive entertainment, finance-only “make money with AI”, or repost channels merely because a title contains “AI”. The creator still needs process-led content that could credibly become a SEELE build-and-play demonstration.

Use platform-native search or official APIs when available. Search-engine results are discovery leads, not proof. Open the profile and validate recent content before inclusion.
